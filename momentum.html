How a month works

The same simple rhythm every month, so you always know what's coming.

  1. We plan

    Your content calendar arrives before the month starts. Nothing is written until you've approved it.

  2. We write

    First drafts come back within 48 hours of each agreed brief.

  3. You review

    Drafts arrive as Google Docs. Leave comments or suggested edits on the page. Every piece includes two rounds of revisions.

  4. You publish

    You post the approved content on your own channels, whenever suits you. We never need your logins.
