Privacy Policy

Last updated: X June 2026

SteadyFlow Content Services ("SteadyFlow", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit this website or contact us, and how we use it.

Who we are

SteadyFlow Content Services is a content writing service for wellness practitioners, operated as a sole trader business in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at info@steadyflow.uk or on 07341 633 649.

What information we collect

This website does not use contact forms, account systems or tracking cookies. We collect personal information only when you choose to give it to us, for example when you:

  • Email us at info@steadyflow.uk — we receive your email address, name and anything you include in your message
  • Call or text us — we receive your phone number
  • Become a client — we collect the business information needed to deliver our services, as agreed with you

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, provide our services, and manage our business relationship with you. The legal bases we rely on under UK GDPR are: taking steps to enter into a contract with you, performing that contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business.

We do not sell your information, share it with third parties for marketing, or send you marketing emails without your consent.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address when the font files are requested. Our email is handled by our email provider. If you become a client, payments may be processed by a payment provider (such as Stripe), which has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. Client records are kept for the duration of our work together and for up to six years afterwards, in line with UK tax record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, to object to or restrict our processing of it, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, email info@steadyflow.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
