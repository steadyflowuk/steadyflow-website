What happens next

We'll also email you within 1 working day to confirm everything.

  1. Onboarding call

    30 minutes by video. We go through your form answers, ask a few follow-up questions and agree what your first month should focus on.

  2. Your first content calendar

    Within 48 hours of the call, you'll get a plan for the month showing what goes out, where and when. Nothing is written until you approve it.

  3. Your first drafts

    Each piece arrives within 48 hours of an agreed brief, as a Google Doc you can comment on.

  4. Review and publish

    Two rounds of revisions per piece, then you post the approved content whenever suits you.
